How to use the Job Switch Worth It Calculator

The Job Switch Worth It Calculator is different from the normal offer comparison tool. It is designed for people who already have a current job and want to know whether a new offer is worth the cost and risk of switching.

1

Enter your current job

Add monthly salary, AWS, bonus, leave, working hours, office days, commute time and current job scores.

2

Enter the new offer

Add the new salary package, annual leave, work pattern, commute and probation-related assumptions.

3

Add switching costs

Include notice buyout, unpaid gap days, one-time costs and whether annual leave can offset the notice period.

4

Review the decision result

Check annual net gain, monthly value difference, break-even months, effective hourly value and risk-adjusted score.

Use Offer Comparison when you are comparing two offers side by side. Use Job Switch Worth It when you are deciding whether to leave your current job for a new offer.

Choose a tool for your next decision

  1. Salary: check one month of gross pay, overtime and estimated CPF. The salary tool uses full CPF rates for eligible citizens and PRs from their third year.
  2. Annual Leave: establish the leave-year balance before discussing unused leave with HR.
  3. Resignation: use confirmed leave and notice terms to itemise the exit settlement.
  4. Offer Comparison: compare two packages over a full year, including AWS and work costs.
  5. Job Switch: add one-off switching costs to compare staying with moving.

A salary increase can shrink after package changes

Illustration: S$4,000 a month with one contractual AWS month gives S$52,000 annual base plus AWS. S$4,200 without AWS gives S$50,400, which is S$1,600 less before CPF, tax, benefits or work costs. Enter written package terms in Offer Comparison before adding exit costs in Job Switch.

Which tool should I start with when resigning?

Start with Annual Leave to reconcile the balance. Then use Resignation for notice dates and settlement. Taking approved leave during notice and using leave to offset notice have different effects; get employer agreement before using an offset.
