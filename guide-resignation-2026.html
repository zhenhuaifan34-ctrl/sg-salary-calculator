Singapore Resignation Guide 2026: Notice, Leave and Final Pay

Updated and source-checked 2026-11-07. Independent educational guide by SG Salary Pro.

Confirm the notice clause, written arrangements and HR leave balance before fixing a new start date. Use the Resignation Calculator to itemise estimates; unresolved terms should stay unconfirmed.

Count notice before applying leave

Use the contract's notice period. Only if the contract is silent, MOM's defaults are one day below 26 weeks' service, one week from 26 weeks to below two years, two weeks from two to below five years, and four weeks from five years. The notice day, weekends and public holidays count. One day's notice ends on the day given.

Approved leave and notice offset are different

ArrangementWhat changes?
Approved annual leave during noticeEmployment continues to the original notice end; attendance may end earlier.
Annual leave offset by agreementThe employment end moves forward. Offset days are not paid again as encashment.

Ask HR to confirm both the formal employment end and the last attendance date. A simple subtraction of five calendar days may not represent five scheduled working days.

Example: leave cash and final-month salary

Assume HR confirms five cash-eligible leave days, monthly gross rate S$4,000 and five working days per week. Daily gross rate = 12 × 4,000 ÷ (52 × 5). Multiplying the unrounded rate by five gives S$923.08 leave encashment.

For a separate incomplete-month example, with 22 scheduled working days and 10 eligible paid days, S$4,000 ÷ 22 × 10 = S$1,818.18. These use different denominators. The two components total S$2,741.26 before other items; the total is an illustration, not a net bank payment.

MOM's gross-rate definition includes qualifying contractual allowances and excludes items such as overtime, bonus, AWS and travel, food or housing allowances. Do not replace gross rate with basic salary automatically or include every allowance.

Confirm the rest of the settlement

Check salary in lieu separately for each party, deductions, expense claims, bonus terms and any tax-clearance requirements. Salary earned while serving notice and salary in lieu have different CPF treatment. Use the CPF guide for the contribution context.

Mistakes to avoid

Encashing the same days used for offset, counting an unknown HR balance as confirmed cash, using the annual-leave daily rate for incomplete-month salary, or treating gross settlement as take-home pay.

Frequently asked question

Can I move my employment end by taking leave?

Approved leave during notice alone does not move it. Notice offset requires agreement. Record which arrangement applies before relying on a date.
