About Us

Welcome to the Singapore Salary & Resignation Calculator Portal.

Our Mission

Navigating labor laws can be daunting for both local employees and HR professionals in Singapore. When reviewing Singapore employment rules—and separate CPF Board contribution rules—we realized there was a critical need for an automated, lightweight, and transparent verification tool.

Why Use Our Tool?

  • Independent estimates: Check each tool’s stated scope and official sources. No government certification or endorsement is claimed.
  • Privacy First: Built using vanilla client-side code. Calculator inputs are processed locally. Copying, downloading or sharing a report is a separate action you choose.
  • HR Ready: Provides a printable personal estimate for discussing assumptions with HR; it is not an official income statement.

Open Source Integrity

This utility portal is operated with independent open-source coding guidelines. We aim to protect workforce rights and streamline monthly salary calculations across cross-border tech and retail communities.
