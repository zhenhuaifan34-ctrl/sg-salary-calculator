Singapore Annual Leave Guide 2026: Entitlement and Proration

Annual leave entitlement is a days calculation. Check your service dates, applicable leave year, contract and HR ledger before converting any balance into cash. The Annual Leave Calculator keeps statutory and company-contract estimates separate.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • Check eligibility and completed service months before prorating leave.
  • Track statutory leave and contractual leave separately.
  • Use HR’s ledger to confirm used days, carry-forward and approved unpaid leave.

Eligibility and the statutory minimum

Employees covered by the Employment Act qualify after at least three months with the employer, including service on probation. The first service year starts on the employment start date.

Proration example: two entitlement tracks

The statutory method uses completed months ÷ 12 × annual entitlement, with fractions below 0.5 rounded down and 0.5 or more rounded up. In a first-service-year example with six completed months, 6 ÷ 12 × 7 = 3.5, rounded to 4 statutory days.

Separately assume a contract grants 14 days a year and uses the same rounding convention: 6 ÷ 12 × 14 = 7 contract days. If HR confirms one valid carried day and two days used, the contract balance is 7 + 1 − 2 = 6 days. Do not add statutory days to contractual days; the statutory track is the minimum check.

Unpaid leave, notice and carry-forward

Approved unpaid leave is excluded when determining completed service months for proration. Normal notice served is included. Partial periods and company leave-year conventions need accurate dates and HR confirmation.

For Part 4 employees, unused statutory leave must be allowed to carry forward to the next 12 months. Treatment above the statutory entitlement, or for employees outside Part 4, depends on the contract. Check expiry and the statutory/company split rather than assuming either “all leave expires” or “all leave lasts forever”.

Reconcile the leave ledger before estimating cash

  • Check the leave year, service start date and entitlement basis.
  • Compare the record of used leave and approved unpaid leave with your payslips.
  • Ask which carried days are still valid and which balance HR confirms for settlement.

Leaving employment?

Use the Resignation Calculator for notice dates and confirmed cash-eligible leave. Taking approved leave during notice and agreeing to offset notice are different arrangements; see the resignation guide.

Common mistakes

Counting every partial calendar month as completed, prorating the whole tenure again after a new leave year begins, double-counting carried leave, or assuming a displayed balance automatically establishes cash eligibility.

Frequently asked question

Does probation delay the three-month statutory test?

No. MOM says the completed-month proration rule applies even while on probation; your contract may provide better benefits.
