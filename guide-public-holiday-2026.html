Singapore Public Holiday Guide 2026: Dates and Pay Scenarios

Updated and source-checked 2026-11-07. Independent educational guide by SG Salary Pro.

First identify whether the holiday falls on a working day, non-working day or rest day in your roster. That choice affects the pay treatment; a generic holiday multiplier can miss it.

For employees whose rest day falls on the Sunday holidays, MOM identifies the following Monday as a public holiday: 1 June, 10 August and 9 November respectively. Use your actual roster, not an assumed Sunday rest day.

Planning ahead? MOM also publishes the 2027 public holiday calendar. Choose the 2027 tab for the dates and accompanying notes.

Apply the right scenario

  • Holiday on a working day, with normal hours worked: by default an extra day's basic pay; monthly gross pay already includes the paid holiday.
  • Holiday on a non-working day: another day off or one day's gross pay in lieu.
  • Holiday on a rest day: the next working day is a paid holiday; work on the rest day has its own pay rules.

Substituting another working day for the public holiday requires mutual agreement. For employees outside Part 4, the employer can offer time off in lieu with an agreed number of hours. Without agreement on the duration, MOM lists three options for the employer: an extra day's basic pay, four hours off for working up to four hours, or a full day off for working more than four hours. Extra hours beyond normal hours may also attract OT when eligible. MOM's public holiday pay rules

Example: normal hours on a working-day holiday

Assume monthly basic pay S$2,600, a five-day week, no substitute holiday agreement, and a holiday that is a normal working day. Daily basic rate = 12 × 2,600 ÷ (52 × 5) = S$120. The additional basic pay is S$120; do not add another full day's gross holiday payment already included in monthly salary. Any OT needs a separate assessment.

Common mistakes

Treating Saturday as everyone's rest day, deducting annual leave for a paid holiday, or using the same basic/gross rate for each scenario. Approved unpaid leave spanning a holiday can affect eligibility; check MOM before final payroll.

For ordinary OT use the Salary Calculator and OT guide. For a holiday-pay ruling use MOM's official calculator linked from its entitlement page; this site does not provide a dedicated holiday-pay calculator.

Frequently asked question

Is the Monday replacement universal?

It follows the rest-day rule. Shift workers should check their roster and the official scenario rather than automatically replacing every Sunday holiday.
