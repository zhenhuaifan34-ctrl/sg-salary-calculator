Singapore Salary Increment Guide 2026: Calculate and Review a Raise

Begin with the amount, effective date and package terms of your own raise. An unsupported industry percentage cannot tell you what an employer has promised or whether your role is paid fairly.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • Calculate the basic-pay percentage and the cash impact for the actual months.
  • Bring comparable occupational wage evidence and your own responsibilities to the pay review.
  • Treat NWC guidance separately from a raise confirmed in writing.

Calculate the percentage and cash difference

Raise percentage = (new basic salary − old basic salary) ÷ old basic salary × 100. The old salary must be positive; a zero starting salary has no defined percentage increase.

Example: S$5,000 to S$5,250 gives S$250 ÷ S$5,000 × 100 = 5%. If effective for all twelve months, the extra base pay is S$3,000. If it starts on 1 July and applies for six full months, the year's extra base pay is S$1,500. A bonus tied to a salary date needs its own contractual calculation.

What current NWC guidance can and cannot tell you

MOM published acceptance of the NWC 2026/2027 Guidelines on 19 October 2026. The announcement encourages wage increases and variable payments calibrated to business performance and prospects. It does not establish one guaranteed raise percentage for every employee. Check the applicable guidelines and any separate PWM/LQS requirement.

Prepare a useful pay-review case

  1. Record changed responsibilities and measurable results since the last review.
  2. Compare equivalent role scope and wage definitions using the salary benchmark method.
  3. Write the requested amount, effective date and whether it changes AWS or other benefits.
  4. If base pay is constrained, compare leave, training or flexibility separately instead of assigning an invented cash value.

Write an evidence line for your review

Record the comparable occupation, the measure, the reference month, the source and your main job duties. For example, the June 2024 MOM dataset gives software developers a median gross monthly wage of S$8,888. Check the published distribution and note why the duties and gross-pay definition do or do not match your role.

Check the effect on spending money

Use the Salary Calculator for its stated CPF and payroll assumptions. Review the income-tax guide separately: a marginal tax band does not apply to all income. The gross increase is not automatically the increase in the bank payment.

Common mistakes

Mixing a part-year raise with a full-year estimate, using total package growth as basic-pay growth, citing a survey forecast as a contractual right, or overlooking a reduced bonus.

Frequently asked question

Is a 5% raise automatically good?

It is the percentage change in the example, not a market verdict. Compare responsibilities, annual package, timing and the evidence for your role; use the Offer Comparison Calculator if considering an alternative.

Before assessing a raise against your occupation, check the latest available monthly wage figures in the Salary Benchmark Explorer.
