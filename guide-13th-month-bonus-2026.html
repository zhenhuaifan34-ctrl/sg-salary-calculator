Singapore AWS Guide 2026: Contract Terms, Proration and CPF

AWS, the “13th-month payment”, is not automatically included in every Singapore salary package. Read the employment contract or collective agreement before counting it as guaranteed income.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • Check the contract or collective agreement for AWS and bonuses.
  • Apply proration only when the written terms support it.
  • Record guaranteed cash and uncertain variable pay separately.

Read the clause before calculating

MOM distinguishes AWS, bonuses and variable payments. These payments are not compulsory unless promised in the applicable terms. Check the amount or formula, qualifying service, payout date, proration and treatment on resignation. A “target bonus” and a promised month of AWS should occupy separate rows in your comparison.

Use the Offer Comparison Calculator to separate guaranteed and assumed amounts; run a zero-variable-bonus case before relying on the headline package.

Build a contract checklist

  • Record the expected payout date and any service-month test.
  • Identify the calculation basis and any rules for probation or resignation.
  • Mark each payment as guaranteed, conditional or an estimate.

Example: a contract-specific nine-month rule

Assume the written policy promises one month's basic salary as AWS, prorated using completed months over twelve, and confirms nine qualifying months. At S$4,000 basic pay, 9 ÷ 12 × 4,000 = S$3,000. This is an illustration of those contract terms. It is not a statutory proration formula or a universal probation rule.

CPF and the bonus month

AWS is generally Additional Wages for CPF. Apply the relevant contribution table and remaining annual AW ceiling, rather than assuming every bonus dollar attracts CPF. For example, S$96,000 of annual CPF-subject OW leaves S$6,000 of the S$102,000 annual salary ceiling; a S$10,000 bonus can exceed that remaining headroom. Read the CPF ceiling explanation.

Before resigning

Ask HR to confirm whether you qualify for a payout and how a notice or payout-date clause applies. Keep an uncertain bonus out of confirmed final settlement. The Resignation Calculator addresses notice and settlement inputs, not an adjudication of bonus eligibility.

Common mistakes

Counting target bonus as guaranteed AWS, adding the same allowance twice, assuming every early departure forfeits AWS, or budgeting gross bonus as cash after CPF and tax.

Frequently asked question

Will I receive prorated AWS if I leave before payout?

Check the actual contract or collective agreement. The arithmetic follows the entitlement terms; a calculator cannot create an entitlement missing from them.
