Singapore Income Tax Guide 2026: Residents and Foreign Employees

Determine tax residency before applying a rate. Citizenship, a work-pass label and CPF eligibility are different from tax residency. A foreign employee can be a Singapore tax resident; check IRAS's conditions for the relevant Year of Assessment (YA).

This is a guide, not a foreigner tax calculator. Use IRAS's residency rules, rate tables and official calculators for your situation.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • Identify your tax residency and Year of Assessment before choosing a rate.
  • Apply progressive rates to slices of chargeable income.
  • Use IRAS for the tax assessment and the Salary Calculator for its separate CPF and pay estimate.

Gross salary, chargeable income and cash are different

Prepare annual employment income, taxable benefits, allowable expenses, eligible deductions and reliefs. Chargeable income is the amount to which the tax bands apply after the applicable adjustments; it is not simply your monthly bank payment multiplied by twelve. Compulsory employee CPF relief has its own eligibility and limits.

Map earnings to the Year of Assessment

YA 2026 generally assesses income earned in calendar year 2025, while YA 2027 generally assesses income earned in 2026. For a foreign employee, IRAS explains the 183-day test, the three-consecutive-year concession and a separate concession for qualifying employment that crosses two calendar years. The two-year concession excludes company directors, public entertainers and professionals. Check your dates and eligibility on the IRAS residency page.

Example: S$40,000 chargeable income

Under resident rates from YA 2024 onward, the first S$20,000 is taxed at 0%, the next S$10,000 at 2%, and the next S$10,000 at 3.5%. On S$40,000 chargeable income, gross tax is S$0 + S$200 + S$350 = S$550, before any applicable rebate. This example begins with chargeable income already established; it does not assume a S$40,000 gross salary has the same result.

Non-resident employment income

IRAS generally taxes non-resident employment income at 15% or the resident-rate calculation, whichever produces the higher tax. Short-term employment exemptions and other income categories have separate conditions. Directors, professionals and entertainers should not apply this simplified employment comparison without checking their category.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Applying the highest marginal rate to every dollar.
  • Assuming no CPF means no income tax.
  • Reusing a past YA's rebate automatically.
  • Treating a monthly payroll estimate as a tax assessment or foreign-employee tax-clearance result.

Use the Salary Calculator for its stated payroll scope and the Offer Comparison Calculator to compare package assumptions; neither establishes your IRAS assessment.

Frequently asked question

Does a pay rise put all my income into the next tax band?

No. Resident rates apply progressively to slices of chargeable income. Recalculate the annual position after checking reliefs, rather than applying one new percentage to the entire salary.
