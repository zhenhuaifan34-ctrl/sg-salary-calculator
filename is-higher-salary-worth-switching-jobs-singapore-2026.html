Higher Salary Offer in Singapore: Switching Costs and Payback

A higher offer is easier to assess after separating recurring gain from one-time transition costs. This guide answers the payback question for someone moving from a current job to a new role; it does not prescribe a minimum raise percentage.

Use the Job Switch Calculator for that decision. For two new offers, use Offer Comparison. For the specific 10% and lost-AWS test, use the 10% scenario guide.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • Separate the recurring cash gain from one-time transition costs.
  • A positive monthly gain lets you estimate the time to recover those costs.
  • Check written notice, start dates and non-cash trade-offs before deciding.

Build a transition ledger

Additional transport and meal costsUnpaid gap between jobs
Changed hours and commute time, shown separatelyContract-confirmed payment lost by leaving

Keep uncertain amounts in a separate scenario. A leave balance is not automatically confirmed cash. Do not count an annual bonus loss both as a lower recurring package and again as a one-time loss.

Worked example: eight months to recover costs

Assume a comparable monthly cash improvement of S$400, S$150 additional recurring transport/meal costs and S$2,000 one-time switching cost. Adjusted monthly gain = 400 − 150 = S$250. Payback = 2,000 ÷ 250 = 8 months. A full twelve months at that gain, less the cost, produces S$1,000 in the first twelve months. A delayed start or unpaid gap would change that result.

These are planning assumptions, not a calculation of a specific person's tax or CPF. If monthly gain is zero or negative, this simple model has no positive payback period. Do not display a negative or infinite number as months to recover the cost.

Check the first twelve months

With the same assumptions and no unpaid gap, the starting cash position is −S$2,000. After the first full month it is −S$1,750, after month 8 it is S$0, and after month 12 it is S$1,000. Count from the actual first paid month and put any unpaid gap in the transition ledger once.

Confirm dates before committing to a start date

Read your notice clause and get any waiver or leave-offset agreement in writing. Taking approved leave during notice does not itself move the employment end; an agreed offset does. Use the Resignation Calculator for confirmed dates and settlement inputs and the Annual Leave Calculator for entitlement days.

Keep non-cash considerations visible

Record manager fit, role scope, flexibility, learning and job stability in words. A cash model cannot establish their value or a probability of probation failure. Compare a confirmed-offer case with a downside case that excludes uncertain payments.

Common mistakes

Using headline salary as cash gain, counting a target bonus as promised, treating employer CPF as spendable cash, or using a short payback period as proof that the role is suitable.

Frequently asked question

Does recovering costs in eight months mean I should accept?

It answers the stated arithmetic only. Review cash reserves, written terms, working time and career considerations before deciding; the calculator is an estimate rather than an employment recommendation.
