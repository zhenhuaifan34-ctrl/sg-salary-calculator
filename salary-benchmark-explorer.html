Methodology and sources

What counts as gross wage?

Basic wage plus overtime, commissions, allowances and other regular cash payments. It is measured before employee CPF and personal income tax and excludes bonuses and employer CPF.

Who is represented?

Full-time resident employees in the published Occupational Wage Survey tables. Check the linked release for detailed coverage and limitations.

How to read the percentiles

P25, median and P75 are the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles. Ties can occur. The numbers describe the surveyed occupation, with all covered industries combined.

Frequently asked questions

Can I search by industry?

Search covers occupation-level wages across covered industries. The browse groups help you find jobs by title. They do not represent industries or provide industry wage figures.

Can I compare take-home pay or annual compensation?

Use comparable gross monthly cash wages for this comparison. For CPF deductions use the Salary Calculator. For bonuses, benefits and costs use Offer Comparison.

Why might my job title be missing?

Employers and MOM can use different job titles. Try a key word or a broader description, and select a listed occupation only if the work matches yours.
