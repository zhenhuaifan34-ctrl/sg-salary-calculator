        Notice and leave examples

        For notice given on 15 November 2026 with one calendar month's notice, the contractual last employment date is 14 December 2026. Notice includes the day it is given and calendar non-working days. This does not identify the last date you attend work.

        For a separate leave-encashment illustration, monthly gross pay of S$3,000 on a five-day week gives 12 × 3,000 ÷ (52 × 5) = S$138.4615 per day. Five confirmed cash-eligible unused days give S$692.31 before applicable CPF and other settlement items. Incomplete-month salary uses the actual month’s working-day denominator instead.

        Does approved leave shorten my notice?

        Approved leave during notice normally keeps the employment end date unchanged. Employer-agreed leave offset can bring the end date forward. Keep cash encashment, leave taken and offset days separate to avoid counting the same days twice.

        Why is the final total withheld?

        Unconfirmed notice treatment, employer calendar, allowances or leave eligibility can change who pays whom. Resolve the flagged inputs rather than treating an incomplete total as a final payroll figure.
