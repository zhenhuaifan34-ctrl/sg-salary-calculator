    What the statutory track uses

    • Employment Act coverage and at least 3 months of employment.
    • 7 days in service year 1, rising by 1 day each year to 14 days from year 8.
    • Completed service months ÷ 12, followed by MOM half-day rounding.
    • A normally served notice period is part of employment; use the agreed employment end date as the cutoff.

    What remains company policy

    Contract entitlement, eligibility, leave-year administration, carry-forward, expiry and manual adjustments depend on employment terms and HR records. The contract estimate stays pending until you actively confirm the available policy choices.

    For resignation encashment amounts, use the Resignation Calculator. Annual Leave calculates entitlement and balance only. Annual Leave does not calculate cash value. Annual Leave does not calculate notice pay, final salary or settlement totals.

    Check the leave balance before using it

    Completed-month example

    A first-year employee covered by the Employment Act with four completed months and a seven-day statutory annual entitlement has 7 × 4 ÷ 12 = 2.33 days, rounded once to two days. With no used leave, carry-forward or adjustments, the statutory balance is two days. A contractual entitlement can be higher and is calculated on its own track.

    What to confirm

    Select the correct leave year, start and calculation dates, and your employer's contractual eligibility and proration policy. Record dated unpaid-leave periods. Unknown policies or partial-month cases can require manual review; a statutory result does not confirm the contract balance or cash eligibility.

    Can I encash the displayed balance?

    This page estimates days, not a cash payment. Confirm cash-eligible leave with HR, then use the Resignation Calculator for gross daily pay and settlement treatment.

    Why can the statutory and contract results differ?

    The statutory minimum follows service years and completed months. Company policy may grant more leave or use a different accrual policy. Do not add the two tracks together.
